Guide: Welcome to this progressive muscle relaxation practice. This exercise will guide you to gently tense and then release different muscle groups, helping your body learn what true relaxation feels like. Find a comfortable position, sitting or lying down. Loosen any tight clothing. Let your arms rest at your sides.

Guide: We will begin with your hands. Breathe in, and make a tight fist with both hands. Squeeze, squeeze, hold it. Notice the tension in your fingers and hands. Now breathe out and release. Let your fingers spread open and relax completely. Notice the difference between tension and relaxation. Let your hands feel heavy and warm.

Guide: Now bring your attention to your forearms and upper arms. Breathe in, and bend your arms, tensing your biceps as if you are showing your muscles. Hold the tension. Feel it in your arms. Now breathe out and release, letting your arms fall back down, completely loose. Notice the pleasant heaviness as the tension drains away.

Guide: Move to your shoulders. Breathe in, and lift your shoulders up toward your ears. Squeeze them tight. Hold it, hold it. Feel all the tension you carry there. Now breathe out and drop your shoulders down. Let them melt away from your ears. Enjoy the feeling of release.

Guide: Now your face. Scrunch up all the muscles in your face. Squeeze your eyes shut, clench your jaw, wrinkle your forehead. Hold that tight, uncomfortable feeling. Now breathe out and release everything. Let your jaw hang slightly open. Smooth your forehead. Let your tongue rest softly. Feel your whole face become calm and smooth.

Guide: Gently press the back of your head into the surface beneath you, tensing your neck. Hold it. Now release, and let your neck and head rest heavy and supported.

Guide: Take a deep, easy breath in, and tighten your chest and stomach muscles. Hold the tension across your torso. Now breathe out and release. Let your belly soften and rise and fall naturally with your breath.

Guide: Now your legs. Straighten them and tense your thigh muscles. Press your knees together slightly. Hold the tension in your thighs. Now release. Let your legs sink heavy into the surface beneath you.

Guide: Point your toes away from you, tensing your calves and feet. Hold it. Feel the tightness. Now breathe out and release. Let your feet relax completely.

Guide: Finally, take a slow breath in, and gently tense your whole body all at once, from your toes to your face. Hold it for a moment. Now breathe out with a sigh, and let everything go. Feel yourself sinking into deep, comfortable relaxation.

Guide: Rest here for a few breaths. Scan your body slowly from your feet to the top of your head. If you notice any spot still holding tension, breathe into that area, and let it soften as you breathe out.

Guide: You have given your body a chance to reset. Remember this feeling of release. Your muscles now know the way back to calm, and you can guide them here whenever you need.

Guide: When you are ready, begin to wake your body gently. Wiggle your fingers and toes. Roll your shoulders slowly. Stretch in any way that feels good. Take one more deep, refreshing breath in, and let it out slowly.

Guide: Well done. Carry this sense of ease with you into the rest of your day.
