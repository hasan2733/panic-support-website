Guide: Welcome to this grounding meditation. When anxiety rises, our attention often gets pulled into worried thoughts about the future. This practice gently brings your attention back to the present moment, using your five senses. You can do this sitting or standing, with your eyes open.

Guide: We will move through five steps together. Take your time with each one. There is no rush.

Guide: Step one. Notice five things you can see. Look slowly around you. Name them quietly in your mind. Perhaps the color of the wall. A shadow on the floor. The shape of a nearby object. Light coming through a window. Just observe, without judging. Five things you can see.

Guide: Step two. Notice four things you can touch or feel. The texture of your clothes against your skin. The surface beneath your hands. The temperature of the air on your face. The weight of your feet on the ground. Four things you can feel, right here, right now.

Guide: Step three. Notice three things you can hear. Listen carefully. Perhaps distant traffic. The hum of an appliance. Your own breathing. A bird outside. You do not need to find pleasant sounds, just notice what is there. Three things you can hear.

Guide: Step four. Notice two things you can smell. Breathe in gently through your nose. Perhaps the scent of your room. A faint smell of food, or soap, or fresh air. If you cannot detect anything strong, simply notice the neutral smell of the air itself. Two things you can smell.

Guide: Step five. Notice one thing you can taste. Perhaps the lingering taste of a recent drink. Or simply the neutral taste inside your mouth. Take a slow sip of water if you have some nearby, and notice it fully. One thing you can taste.

Guide: Now pause. You have just traveled through all five senses. Notice where your attention is now. It is here, in this room, in this moment. The worried thoughts may still be nearby, but you are anchored in the present.

Guide: Let us do one more gentle round, a little quicker this time. Name five things you see. Four things you feel. Three things you hear. Two things you smell. One thing you taste. Take your time.

Guide: Feel your feet on the ground. Feel the steadiness of the surface holding you up. You are here. You are safe in this moment.

Guide: Anxiety tells us we are in danger, but right now, in this present moment, look around you. You are okay. This feeling will pass, as all feelings do.

Guide: When you are ready, take one slow, full breath in, and let it out completely. Gently stretch, or simply sit quietly for a moment longer.

Guide: Well done. You can return to this five senses practice anytime you need to come back to the present.
