Guide: Welcome to this calm breathing practice. Find a comfortable position, sitting or lying down. Let your shoulders drop away from your ears. You can keep your eyes open with a soft gaze, or gently close them, whichever feels safer for you right now.

Guide: We are going to breathe together, slowly and gently. There is nothing to force here. Your breath does not need to be deep. Just let it be easy.

Guide: Begin by breathing in through your nose for a slow count of four. One. Two. Three. Four. Now let the breath flow out through your mouth for a slow count of six. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six.

Guide: Again, breathe in. One. Two. Three. Four. And breathe out, letting your body soften. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six.

Guide: Notice the gentle rise of your chest, and the soft fall as you breathe out. Each out-breath is a chance to let go of a little tension.

Guide: Breathe in. One. Two. Three. Four. Breathe out. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six.

Guide: If your mind wanders to worried thoughts, that is completely normal. You do not need to fight the thoughts. Just notice them, and kindly return your attention to the breath.

Guide: Breathe in. One. Two. Three. Four. Breathe out. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six.

Guide: Feel your feet resting on the ground, or your body supported by the chair beneath you. You are safe in this moment.

Guide: Breathe in. One. Two. Three. Four. Breathe out, a little longer this time. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six.

Guide: Let your jaw relax. Let the small muscles around your eyes soften. There is nowhere else you need to be.

Guide: Breathe in. One. Two. Three. Four. Breathe out. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six.

Guide: You are doing well. There is no perfect way to do this. Simply being here, breathing, is enough.

Guide: Breathe in. One. Two. Three. Four. Breathe out. One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Six.

Guide: Now, for these last few breaths, breathe at your own natural pace. No counting. Just gentle, easy breaths, in and out.

Guide: Notice how your body feels now, compared to when we began. Perhaps a little calmer. Perhaps a little softer. Whatever you feel is okay.

Guide: When you are ready, slowly bring your awareness back to the room around you. Wiggle your fingers and toes. Take one more full, easy breath in, and let it go.

Guide: Well done. You gave yourself these minutes of calm, and you can return to this practice whenever you wish.
